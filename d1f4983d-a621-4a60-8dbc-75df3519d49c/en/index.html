Flow 1

Product & price flow

Product and price data can be fed from Qube ERP via integration, or created and managed directly in PIM — both models are supported and can run together.

Source: Qube ERPproduct & price sync (optional)
→
PIMproduct & price master data · variants · taxonomy
→
Publish & rulesdata quality · price & campaign checks
→
Webshop & Appshopsales channels
  • Two feeding models. Products and prices can either sync automatically via Qube ERP integration, or be created manually/in bulk directly in PIM; both can be used together.
  • Centralized price management. List price, discounted price and campaign prices are managed from a single point in PIM; channel- and segment-based pricing is supported.
  • Publishing rules & sync. Product/price synchronization runs via scheduled jobs (at defined times) or on-demand manual triggers; products missing images, price or category information are not published.

Flow 2

Order & fulfillment flow

The entire lifecycle from cart to delivery; each step triggers the next via events.

Webshop & Appshopcart & checkout
→
Payment GWcollection · fraud check
→
OMSorder capture · sourcing · stock reservation
→
WMSpicking · packing · shipping
→
TMScarrier selection · label · tracking
→
Deliverycustomer notification
  • Dynamic sourcing. OMS routes the order to the right warehouse based on cost, stock and capacity; it splits it if needed (split shipment).
  • Status management. Order, cancellation and return statuses are managed by a rule engine; every transition is reflected to the webshop and the customer.
  • Returns line. Reverse logistics runs back through the same chain: TMS return shipment → WMS goods receipt → OMS stock & return approval → Payment GW refund.

Qube ERPaccounting entry · reconciliation
  • E-waybill. When a shipment is finalized in WMS, the waybill is automatically created in Logo and ready before loading onto the vehicle.
  • E-invoice / e-archive. The correct document type is selected automatically by customer type (corporate/individual); the invoice is sent to the customer by email.
  • Reconciliation. Order, collection and invoice data are matched in Qube ERP with daily reconciliation reports; discrepancies are detected automatically.
  • Cancellation & return documents. When a return is approved, the return invoice runs back through the same line.

Flow 4

Stock & price synchronization

Single source of truth principle: price comes from Qube, physical stock is managed in WMS, and salable stock is calculated in OMS.

WMSphysical stock · warehouse movements
→
OMS Inventorysalable stock · reservation · blocking
→
Channel stockchannel-based rules & buffer
→
Webshop & Appshopreal-time availability
Real-time

WMS movements (goods receipt, count, shipment) are instantly reflected to OMS inventory; the webshop never shows stale stock.

Bidirectional

Price lists are taken from Qube ERP; sales, returns and stock movements are fed back to Qube ERP.

Safety

Oversell risk is minimized with channel-based safety stock and a rule engine.

Confidential 13 / 19
Integration architecture

One hub, resilient integrations

All external system connections are managed through the Integration & Orchestration Manager — no point-to-point connections.

Qube ERP

Product · price · accounting

Product master data and price lists inbound; sales, returns and stock movements outbound. Batch initial load + event-based delta synchronization.

Logo

E-waybill · e-invoice

Shipment and invoicing events trigger document creation; document statuses (approved, rejected) are fed back.

PSPs & banks

Payment Gateway

Multi-PSP support, dynamic routing and failover; collection reconciliation is automatic.

Carriers

TMS hub

20+ ready-made carrier integrations; rule-based dispatch, label generation and end-to-end tracking.

Resilience: Integration-sourced single points of failure are eliminated with an auto-fix module, failover management and detailed logging.
