Orders, returns and exchanges · RFP §5.1.2.5–5.1.2.9

The order lives in the OMS, the customer sees it on one screen

What the platform does
  • Digital orders (web, mobile, marketplace) are created on the platform and handed to the OMS by event as soon as they exist; nothing closes before delivery confirmation.
  • Unified Order History: web, mobile, marketplace and OMS-created store orders in one list for the customer; statuses flow back from the OMS.
  • Shipping information comes from the OMS; tracking number and status on the customer screen and in notifications (JetSMS, e-mail, push).
  • Return and exchange requests open on the platform: full/partial return, full/partial exchange, size, colour and product exchange; return eligibility from PIM.
What the OMS owns, we display
  • Order orchestration, allocation, packing and shipping, carrier integrations, store orders, available inventory.
  • Exception management: shipping damage, operational error, wrong item, non-returnable items and satisfaction cases under OMS rules; authorised overrides in the audit trail.
  • Status flow-back: return and exchange status updates to the platform by event; invoices hourly; customer notifications triggered from the platform.
  • Control Tower reports and fixes every difference between the two sides (in channel but not in OMS, cancelled in OMS but open in channel).

Overselling prevention: the sales decision uses only OMS available inventory; orders not yet in the OMS and pending orders are deducted on the platform side; at campaign peaks and integration delays critical stock is closed to the channel on the fast lane.
